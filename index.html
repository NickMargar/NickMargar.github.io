Facts Stats - Terms of Service and Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Facts Stats is a private, non-commercial tool used only by the owner of the TikTok accounts

"Pixels and Facts", "Pixels and Facts GR", "Doodles and Facts" and "Doodles and Facts GR".

It is not offered to the public.

Terms Of Service

• The tool reads basic profile information and video statistics (views, likes, comments, shares) of the owner's own TikTok accounts, after the owner authorizes it through TikTok Login Kit.

• At the owner's request it may upload the owner's own videos to their TikTok drafts. It does not edit or delete content, and does not access other users' accounts.

• It is provided as is, with no warranty. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions.

Privacy Policy

• Data collected: the account's display name/open ID and public statistics of its own videos.

• Use: only to compare how the owner's videos perform. Data is stored locally on the owner's computer, is never sold or shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising.

• Retention: kept until the owner deletes it. Revoking the app's access stops all collection.

• No data from other TikTok users is collected.

• Contact: the account owner, via the TikTok accounts listed above.
