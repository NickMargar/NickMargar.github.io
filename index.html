Facts Stats

Home · Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

Facts Stats is a private desktop tool that publishes my own short educational videos (original pixel-art and hand-drawn animations) to my own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok channels, and collects their view, like and watch-time statistics so I can compare which styles and languages work best.

Channels: Pixels and Facts, Pixels and Facts GR, Doodles and Facts, Doodles and Facts GR.

It has a single user (the channel owner) and is not offered to the public. It uses the YouTube API Services, the Instagram API and the TikTok API.
