Facts Stats - Privacy Policy

Home · Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Facts Stats is a private, non-commercial desktop tool used only by the owner of the channels "Pixels and Facts", "Pixels and Facts GR", "Doodles and Facts" and "Doodles and Facts GR" on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

• Google/YouTube: with the scopes youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly it reads the authorized channel's ID and title, the list of its own videos and their statistics (views, likes, comments, average view duration/percentage, traffic sources). With youtube.upload it uploads videos chosen by the owner to the owner's channel, with the owner's title and description.

• Instagram and TikTok: the account's ID/username and statistics of its own posts; uploads of the owner's own videos.

How the data is used, stored and shared

• Data used: the authorized channel's ID/name and statistics of its own videos, plus the video files and texts the owner chooses to upload.

• Use: only to publish the owner's videos and compare how they perform. Data is stored locally on the owner's computer, is never sold or shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising. No cookies or tracking.

• YouTube data is accessed through the YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy.

• Retention and deletion: stored data can be deleted at any time by deleting the local database; it is kept no longer than needed for the comparison. Access can be revoked at any time: Google at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions, TikTok in Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions, Instagram in Settings > Apps and websites.

• No data from other users is collected.

• Contact: the channel owner, via the channels listed above.
